🔄 End-to-End Decision Request Lifecycle

1

Shopper Intent & Interaction Telemetry

Shopper browses 32 catalog items or sets explicit intent (e.g., "Productivity"). Intentional pointer dwell (≥900ms) or detail modal views emit privacy-preserving anonymous events to SQLite (`session_events`).

2

Engine A: Hybrid Candidate Generation & Ranking

Evaluates multi-item cart anchors or cold-start fallback. Computes 14-feature candidate representations, Apriori co-occurrence lift (980 rules), supervised HistGradientBoosting probabilities, and MMR diversity reranking (λ=0.75).

3

Engine B: Constrained Economic Margin Optimization

Calculates exact integer-cent line-item margin against active merchant settings (`margin_floor` ≥ 15%, `discount_cap` ≤ 20%). Recommends bundle discounts if safe; falls back gracefully to "Standard price · No discount applied" if margin breached.

4

Engine C: Dual-Mode Customer-Facing Explanation

Google Gemini 2.5 Flash explains why the bundle was recommended using physical product attributes. Enforces 3.5s timeout, response cache, and sub-5ms deterministic offline fallback with immutable financial prices.
